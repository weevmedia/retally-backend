1

Scan your receipt

Tap the scan button and hold your phone over the receipt. ReTally finds the edges and takes the photo for you.

2

Every line is read

Store, date, each item and its price are pulled out automatically. You can fix anything before saving.

3

Double charges are flagged

The same item at the same price showing up twice gets marked, so you can ask the store for it back.

4

Your budget stays on track

Each receipt adds to this month's spending. Home shows where you'll land by month-end, in green, amber or red.

Tips for a good scan

  • Lay the receipt flat on a dark surface.
  • Use good light and avoid shadows across the text.
  • Fit the whole receipt in the frame. For long ones, scan the top and bottom.

Delete your ReTally account

ReTally is published by Weev Media.
Two ways to delete your account
  • In the app: open ReTally and go to More → Account → Delete account, then confirm.
  • Without the app: email support-retally@weevmedia.com with the subject "Delete my ReTally account". Send it from the email address you sign in with, or include your account ID from More → Account, so we can confirm the account is yours. We reply within 2 business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your ReTally account and the data linked to it:

  • Your account: name, email address and sign-in details
  • Receipts you backed up, including their line items, prices and store details
  • Receipt photos stored in your backup
  • Budgets, shopping lists and favourite stores
  • Records of the devices you used ReTally on

Data stored only on your phone (for example, receipts scanned as a guest) is removed from that phone when you delete your account in the app, or when you uninstall ReTally.

What we keep, and for how long

Your data is deleted from our systems within 30 days of your request. We keep information only where we must, to meet a legal obligation, resolve a dispute or prevent abuse, and only for as long as that requires.

Ad partners (Google AdMob, Unity Ads) handle the data they collect under their own policies. To reset your advertising ID, see Ads and your choices.

Just want to remove some data?

You can delete individual receipts in the app, or turn off Cloud backup in More so new receipts stay on your phone. See Your rights and choices in our Privacy Policy.

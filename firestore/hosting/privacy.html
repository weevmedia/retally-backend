Privacy Policy

The short version
  • ReTally reads your store receipts so you can track spending, spot double charges, plan your shopping and catch recalls and price drops.
  • As a guest, your receipts stay on your phone. If you sign in and keep cloud backup on, they're stored in your account so you can restore them on another device.
  • Some features send receipt text or photos to our servers and to an AI service to read them. We never sell your personal information for money.
  • The app is free and shows ads. You control ad tracking with your device's privacy settings and the consent screen.
  • You can delete receipts, turn off backup, or delete your account at any time in the app.

1. Who we are

ReTally (the "App") is made and operated by Weev Media ("we", "us", "our"), based in Massachusetts, USA. This policy explains what information the App and our related websites and services (together, the "Services") collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the ReTally apps for iPhone and Android.

If you have questions, contact us at support-retally@weevmedia.com.

What we try not to collect

We do not want full payment card numbers. Before receipt text is stored in the cloud, the App removes anything that looks like a full card number. Please don't scan documents other than store receipts.

Guests vs. signed-in users

As a guest, your receipts, photos and lists are stored only on your device and are automatically removed after 30 days. Some features still send receipt content to our servers to work (for example reading a damaged receipt or naming items — see AI processing). If you sign in and cloud backup is on, your receipts and receipt photos are also stored in our cloud database so they can be restored on your other devices.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the App: read receipts, list items, find duplicate charges, track spending and budgets, build your Smart List, back up and restore your receipts, and sync between your devices.
  • Price-drop alerts: compare prices you paid with newer prices seen at the same store chain, and tell you if an item got cheaper.
  • Food recall alerts: check recalls published by the U.S. FDA and USDA against items you bought.
  • Nearby-store reminders: remind you to check your list when you're near a store (see Location).
  • Sign-in and security: send one-time sign-in codes by email, verify Apple and Google sign-ins, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the Services secure.
  • Support and quality: answer your questions, fix crashes and bugs, and improve reading accuracy and features. Weev Media, and anyone we authorize to help run the Services, may view account and receipt data when needed to provide support, investigate problems or abuse, or meet legal obligations.
  • Ads: show ads that keep the App free (see Ads).
  • Legal: comply with law, enforce our Terms of Use, and protect our rights and the rights of others.

4. AI processing

Some features use Google's Gemini AI, called from our servers (never directly from your device):

  • Reading damaged receipts: if the App can't fully read a receipt on your device, the receipt photo is sent to our server and to Gemini to read it.
  • Categories and item names: receipt line descriptions (for example "KS ALMONDS") and the store name are sent to identify the product type and a plain name.
  • Smart List and tips: your purchase history is analyzed on our servers to suggest what to buy and to show tips on your Home screen.

AI results can be wrong — please check important details against your receipt. Google processes this content as our service provider under its API terms; we do not allow it to be used to identify you.

5. De-identified and aggregated data

To make features work for everyone, we keep some information in a form that doesn't identify you:

  • a shared dictionary of receipt line descriptions and their product name and category, without any account information;
  • prices, items and dates from receipts, used to tell shoppers when the same item became cheaper at the same store chain. Other users only ever see a price, never who paid it.

We don't try to re-identify de-identified data, and we require the same of anyone we share it with.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that run the Services for us under contract: Google Cloud and Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage, server functions, hosting, Crashlytics crash reporting), Google Gemini API (AI processing), Resend (sending sign-in code emails), Apple and Google (Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, push and location services on your device), and Google AdMob (ads).
  • Advertising partners through Google AdMob, as described in Ads.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of users, us or others.
  • Business transfers: if the App moves to a company we form (for example a Weev Media LLC), or is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that change, subject to this policy. We'll update this policy to name the new operator.
  • With your permission: for example when you share a receipt photo using your phone's share sheet.

7. Ads and your choices

ReTally is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions to show, limit and measure ads, and — only where you allow it — to personalize them.

  • iPhone: the App asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", AdMob won't use your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

8. Location and notifications

Nearby-store reminders are off until you turn them on. When on, the App uses your phone's built-in location features to set up reminder zones around stores from your receipts and to notice when you arrive. This works in the background, including when the App is closed, which is why it asks for "Always" / "Allow all the time" access. Your location is used on your device for this; we don't upload your location history to our servers. To find a store's position, the store address printed on your receipt is converted to coordinates by your phone's mapping service (Apple or Google).

You can turn reminders off in the App (More → Notify me near stores) or remove location or notification permission in your phone's settings at any time.

9. How long we keep information

  • Guest receipts: stored on your device only, and removed automatically after 30 days.
  • Signed-in, backed-up receipts and photos: kept until you delete them or delete your account. Deleted receipts are removed from the cloud within 30 days.
  • Account data: kept while your account exists; deleted within 30 days after you delete your account, except where we must keep something to meet a legal obligation, resolve disputes or prevent abuse.
  • Sign-in codes: expire within minutes and are stored only in hashed form.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • De-identified data (section 5) may be kept longer because it doesn't identify you.

10. Your rights and choices

  • In the App: edit or delete receipts; turn cloud backup off; turn nearby reminders off; log out; or delete your account (More → Account → Delete account), which deletes your account and the data stored with it.
  • By email: ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by writing to support-retally@weevmedia.com from the email on your account (or include your account ID from More). We'll verify the request and reply within 30 days (45 days where state law allows).
  • Device settings: control camera, photos, location, notifications and ad tracking permissions at any time.

We won't treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

11. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information and delete your information;
  • opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising, and of profiling with legal or similarly significant effects (we don't do such profiling);
  • appeal if we decline your request (reply to our decision email with "Appeal").

We don't sell personal information for money. Allowing AdMob to use your advertising identifier for personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some laws; you can opt out using the device settings in Ads or by emailing us. We honor Global Privacy Control signals where they apply. In the last 12 months we collected the categories listed in section 2, for the purposes in section 3, and disclosed them to the recipients in section 6. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask them to prove they're authorized.

12. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls that limit each account to its own data, staff access limited to authorized people, with changes logged, and Google Cloud's security infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and email account secure, and tell us right away if you think your account was misused.

13. Children

ReTally is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

14. Where ReTally is offered

ReTally is offered only in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the App while traveling, it is still handled under this policy.
